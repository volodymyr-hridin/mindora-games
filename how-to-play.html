How to Play Mindora Games

Three quick games. No registration. Free to play in your browser.

Pattern Quest

Study a grid of symbols and choose the missing symbol from four answers. Complete five puzzles to get a daily score out of 500. Your answers are saved in your browser, and you can share a spoiler-free score with friends.

Number Trail

Find 1 on the board, then select 2, 3 and continue up to 15. Each next number must be directly above, below, left or right of the previous number. Finish to record your time.

Memory Match

Turn over two cards. If they match, they remain face up. Find all eight pairs with as few attempts as possible. You can replay to improve your score.

Frequently asked questions

Are the games free?

Yes, all three games are currently free to play.

Do I need an account?

No. Your progress is stored in your browser. Clearing site data or changing devices can reset it.

When do daily puzzles change?

Daily challenges use UTC dates and update at 00:00 UTC.

Can I share my result?

Yes. Use the Share or Copy button at the end of a game to challenge a friend.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes. The games are designed to work in modern mobile browsers.
