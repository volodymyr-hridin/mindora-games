Which game should you start with?

New to puzzles? Choose Easy Sudoku if you like numbers, or Easy Memory Match if you prefer pictures. Want a quick challenge? Try a 30-second Fruit Rush round. Like daily habits? Pattern Quest offers a fresh daily set, and you can check your score against your previous results.

All of these are playable in a browser. Game progress and best scores are saved locally where supported, so using a different phone or clearing browser storage can affect saved results.

Practical tips for playing on mobile

  1. Use a modern browser. Open a game directly instead of installing an unknown APK or extension.
  2. Start in relaxed or easy mode. Learn how controls respond before trying a faster or harder round.
  3. Keep your connection available. These are web pages, so do not assume they work offline.
  4. Take a break when you need one. Choose a short timed arcade round if you only have a minute, or return to a saved Sudoku puzzle when you have more time.

Frequently asked questions

Are these online games free in India?

Yes. The games listed here are free to play in your browser, with no required account.

Do I need to download an app?

No. Open a game link using a modern browser on your phone, tablet or computer.

Can I play these games in Hindi?

The Mindora interfaces listed here currently use English. We do not claim to have Hindi versions yet.

Will my scores sync between different phones?

Do not rely on automatic sync. Local records can remain on one browser and may be lost when its storage is cleared.
