Hole World: grow your portal without wasting time

The first few seconds matter most. A small portal cannot swallow every object, so an efficient route is more useful than chasing the biggest building on the map.

Your objective

Steer the portal through a miniature 3D city, collect objects that fit, grow the opening and reach the level goal before the timer expires. Each city is another opportunity to improve your route.

Play Hole World

1. Start with clusters of small objects

At the beginning of a round, ignore anything wider than your opening. Look for groups of small collectibles in an open area. Short movements between nearby objects reduce travel time and help you grow quickly.

2. Steer with deliberate, short adjustments

On a phone, hold and drag on the game field to guide the portal. On desktop, use mouse dragging or the keyboard controls shown in the game. Small corrections help when approaching a narrow group of objects; larger movements are better for crossing open streets.

3. Revisit bigger targets after growing

When an object does not fit, do not keep pushing against it. Remember its location, collect smaller items and return once the opening has expanded. This avoids wasting valuable seconds.

4. Plan a loop, not a zigzag

Pick a cluster, clear it and move toward the next nearby cluster. Repeatedly crossing the entire map costs time. When a road divides two neighborhoods, sweep one side before switching to the other.

5. Keep an eye on the goal and timer

Early in the round, prioritize growth. Later, when the goal is close, choose the quickest reachable objects rather than a distant landmark. If a route is not working, restart and try a different opening path.

Challenge yourself

Play the same difficulty twice. In your second attempt, begin with the nearest cluster of small objects and compare how quickly your portal grows.
