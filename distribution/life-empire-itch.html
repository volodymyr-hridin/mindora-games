    How to play Life Empire

    Choose a character, a personal dream and a market scenario. Each turn advances one month: earn a salary, manage energy and living costs, invest in rental homes or businesses, and respond to economic events. The goal is to cover your expenses with passive income for three consecutive months while keeping cash positive.

    You can play free without registering. Your progress and personal best are stored locally on this device. Try a different profession or scenario to discover another route to financial freedom.

    Life Empire is a fictional strategy game, not investment advice. Free to play, no required account. Progress stays in this browser. Privacy.
