## Upload packages
- **itch.io:** `distribution/life-empire-itch.html` is a self-contained HTML5 build. Select HTML Game, upload the HTML file, preview on desktop and mobile, then choose public visibility. A creator account is required.
- **CrazyGames:** Basic Launch may accept a build without SDK but has a quality review and no monetization in Basic Launch. A platform-specific package and testing are needed before submission. Full Launch requires SDK and additional compliance.
- **Poki:** Developer submission and editorial review are required. Prepare screenshots, a trailer, mobile/iframe QA and a Poki-compatible build.
- **GameDistribution and other portals:** Confirm current SDK/ad requirements and licensing before sending a build. Avoid signing exclusivity agreements without review.

## Launch QA still required
- Real-device touch and portrait/landscape checks, plus embedded iframe sizing.
- Gameplay usability sessions with first-time players and accessibility/keyboard checks.
- Full privacy/cookie review and monetization eligibility review.
- Verify actual GA4 game events after consent, not just code presence.
- Production analytics and retention cohorts once real traffic arrives.

## Monetization
No revenue is guaranteed. The Mindora site contains an ads.txt publisher identifier, but its presence does not prove AdSense approval. Do not insert live ads until the account and game-specific placements are confirmed compliant. Prioritize engaged sessions, organic search, repeat play, and review-based portal distribution.
