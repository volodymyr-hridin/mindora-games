About Mindora Games

Mindora Games is an independent project building short, accessible brain games for everyday play. Our aim is simple: make a few minutes of thinking fun.

What can you play?

Choose from six free browser games: Pattern Quest for visual puzzles, Sudoku for number logic, Memory Match for card matching, Fruit Slash for fast swipes, Fruit Rush for reflexes, and Hole World for 3D arcade fun.

Play without an account

Games are free to start on mobile and desktop. Some games save scores and progress locally in your browser. Clearing browser storage can remove this progress.
