About Mindora Games

Mindora Games is an independent project building short, accessible brain games for everyday play. Our aim is simple: make a few minutes of thinking fun.

What can you play?

Pattern Quest challenges your ability to spot patterns. Number Trail tests your ability to follow an adjacent number path. Memory Match rewards attention and recall.

Early access

Mindora is an evolving project. We are improving game variety, accessibility and difficulty. There are no paid memberships or advertising in the current test version.
