How to play

  1. Move the hole across the ground using the controls shown in the game.
  2. Start by collecting objects small enough to fit into the opening.
  3. As the hole grows, look for larger objects and keep collecting to reach your goal.

Tips for a better score

Begin in areas with clusters of small items. Avoid wasting time on objects that are still too big for your hole.
