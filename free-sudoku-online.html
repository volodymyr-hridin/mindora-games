How to play

  1. Fill every row with the digits 1 to 9, without repeating a number.
  2. Fill every column with the digits 1 to 9, without repeating a number.
  3. Each 3×3 box must also contain the digits 1 to 9 exactly once.

Tips for a better score

Start with rows, columns or 3×3 boxes that already contain many numbers. Use notes to track candidates rather than guessing.
