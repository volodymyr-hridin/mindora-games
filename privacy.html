Privacy Notice

Last updated: October 21, 2026.

Mindora Games is currently an early-access website offering free browser games. This notice describes the website as currently implemented. It will be updated before advertising or account features are introduced.

Local game progress

The games use your browser's localStorage to remember scores, streaks and daily puzzle completion. These values remain in your browser and can be removed by clearing this site's browser data. They are not used for cross-site advertising.

Hosting

The website is deployed through Vercel, which may process technical connection data such as IP addresses to deliver and secure the site. See Vercel's privacy policy.

Advertising and analytics

Google Analytics 4 is integrated to measure page visits and game interactions. Google AdSense advertising is not currently integrated. Before ads are enabled, we will update this notice and implement the appropriate consent-management solution.

Contact and controller details

Operator identity, contact address and any legally required controller information will be published before the commercial launch. This early-access notice is not a substitute for a complete legal privacy policy.

Google Analytics 4

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand page visits and interactions with our games, such as starting or completing a game. Google Analytics may use cookies or similar technologies. Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy. We do not intentionally send player names or game answers to Google Analytics. Local game progress remains in your browser.

Analytics consent controls are being reviewed for the commercial launch. This notice alone does not replace consent where required.
