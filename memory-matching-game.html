Free Memory Matching Game

Challenge your concentration in a quick card-matching game. Turn over two cards at a time and remember where each picture appears.

Play Memory Match →

How to play

  1. Select any two face-down cards to reveal their pictures.
  2. Matching cards stay visible. Non-matching cards turn face down again.
  3. Find all pairs and try to finish with fewer moves on your next round.

Tips for a better score

Remember the positions of cards you have already seen. Focus on matching known pairs instead of flipping random cards.
